# Changelog

## 0.4.1

- **Lighter `/npd melee log`**: while it is not recording, the addon no longer prepares its log lines on every melee range change.

## 0.4.0 - First release

- **Distance on every nameplate**, as a range such as `20-25 yd`, bracketed with your own spells (talents included), a few items and the interaction distances. Party and raid members get an exact number.
- **Real melee range for your target**, read from the melee abilities on your action bars, so it grows with the size of big enemies and while you both move.
- **Colors by class**, set up from your spells and talents: hunters (melee, dead zone, shooting range, Hunter's Mark), warriors, rogues and druids in cat or bear form (melee, charge range), and every other class. Druids keep separate rows for each form. You can edit any row.
- **Display options**: where the text sits on the health bar, font size and outline, the number format, coloring the text and the unit's name, which units to show, and how often it updates.
- **Translated into every client language**: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Korean, Portuguese, Russian and Chinese.
