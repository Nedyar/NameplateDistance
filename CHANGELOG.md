# Changelog

## 0.5.0

- **Colors of their own for friendly and neutral units**: green up close to red past your longest helpful spell, set up from the checks that work on friendly units. The Colors page switches between the enemies' rows and theirs.
- **Druids edit any form's colors from any form**: the Colors page picks caster, cat or bear form for the enemies' rows, without shifting.
- **Two reset buttons**: Reset these colors sets up again the rows shown; Reset all colors, every set of rows of the character.
- **Mouseover range icon** (Action Bars, off by default): while the mouse is over a unit, action buttons with a key bound show a small mouse icon, in the key's color when the unit is within that ability's range and red when it is not.
- Helpful spells that never answer for a unit (Eagle Eye, Aspect of the Monkey) no longer count as range checks.

## 0.4.2

- **Right distances for friendly units**: spells cast on your pet (Dismiss Pet, Mend Pet, Eyes of the Beast...) answer for your pet, not for the unit asked about, so friendly units far away showed 8-10 yd (0-10 yd in combat). They are no longer used to measure.
- **`/npd check` opens a window** you can copy the report from, like `/npd melee`.

## 0.4.1

- **Lighter `/npd melee log`**: while it is not recording, the addon no longer prepares its log lines on every melee range change.

## 0.4.0 - First release

- **Distance on every nameplate**, as a range such as `20-25 yd`, bracketed with your own spells (talents included), a few items and the interaction distances. Party and raid members get an exact number.
- **Real melee range for your target**, read from the melee abilities on your action bars, so it grows with the size of big enemies and while you both move.
- **Colors by class**, set up from your spells and talents: hunters (melee, dead zone, shooting range, Hunter's Mark), warriors, rogues and druids in cat or bear form (melee, charge range), and every other class. Druids keep separate rows for each form. You can edit any row.
- **Display options**: where the text sits on the health bar, font size and outline, the number format, coloring the text and the unit's name, which units to show, and how often it updates.
- **Translated into every client language**: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Korean, Portuguese, Russian and Chinese.
